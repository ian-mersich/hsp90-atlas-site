# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.391

This release updates the reviewed HSP90–gene evidence while retaining the previously reviewed co-chaperone and HSP90-cycle views.

- The HSP90–gene core contains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,027 source records. Some records represent the same paper evaluated separately for different isoforms; they are not 4,027 independent articles.
- Primary-source re-review resolved 155 pairs that previously had only findings marked for recheck. Twenty-five pairs retain a supported named-isoform observation. For 124 pairs the reviewed sources do not support the named pair, and six contain relevant context without a pair-specific interaction. The original source findings remain available where they also support other pairs.
- Nine additional reviewed observations cover extracellular HSP90α signaling, HSP90AB1–DSCC1 cellular association, and IL-6 regulation of the HSP90β promoter. None establishes a new HSP90 client.
- The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90-cycle model retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence and does not add bulk unreviewed database observations.
- The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients are withheld from this release's public files and displays. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status. Corrected CPTAC results remain private pending a separate publication review.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association or source finding does not establish that two proteins do not interact. Evidence profiles and network views are exploratory; this release does not assign final client status, definitive rankings, or pathway interpretations.
