# HSP90 Atlas release changelog

## Atlas v1.6.443 — 2026-10-05

Three source-linked partner findings are added to the Interactive HSP90 Cycle's additional relationships. Purified human CHIP binding to a defined HSPA1A C-terminal EEVD peptide is distinguished from full-length cellular occupancy (PMID 35148989). Mouse ERdj3–BiP binding (PMID 21217698) and hamster ERj3–BiP binding (PMID 26085089) retain their experimental species. These findings do not establish a timed HSP90 Cycle step, GRP94 handoff, or HSP90 client relationship. The 165 relationships in the timed Cycle and their existing findings remain unchanged.

The core retains all 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,142 source findings from Data v6.441. Numerical profiles, scores, ranks, readiness decisions, client assignments and the 132 historical recheck-impact mappings remain unchanged. The existing LDLR score-input exclusion and AIP scoring limits remain in force. The co-chaperone companion remains at v6.301, and detailed HPA and BioGRID/IntAct profiles remain at v6.109 over the historical v6.104 pair universe. Affected HPA/CPTAC summaries and private bulk Cycle observations remain withheld.

UI 0.11.13 keeps peptide and species qualifications visible beside these additional relationships. The website, reviewed Cycle and downloads share Atlas v1.6.443 as the current public release.
