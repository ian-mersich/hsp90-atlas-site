# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.403

This release incorporates the v6.402 organelle review and adds three source-reviewed cytosolic HSP90 observations from three primary studies. The organelle review adds HSP90B1/GRP94 associations with ERGIC1, ITIH3, SEC61B, and TM9SF4, and TRAP1 associations with AFG3L2, DLD, DNAJA3, IARS2, and TIMM44; these proximity, co-recovery, or cross-link measurements remain assay associations rather than direct binding or client claims. HSP90AA1–MAVS is a constrained hepatocyte/HBV-model candidate-client observation and remains support-only; the tagged co-immunoprecipitation and non-selective pharmacology do not establish alpha-specific client dependence. HSP90AA1–USP9X is a disease-model-specific upstream stability mechanism, supported by co-immunoprecipitation, cycloheximide chase, and MG132 rescue; it does not establish direct binding or a USP9X client relationship. Extracellular MMP2 association is visible in the HSP90AA1 and HSP90AB1 views because both cytosolic isoforms were compared in conditioned medium; it remains a non-exclusive assay association, not beta preference, direct binding, or client evidence.

The public core retains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and contains 4,064 source findings. There are 2,748 source-reviewed pairs, of which 2,265 have pair-specific support. The reviewed interaction network contains 2,263 source-backed links. The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90 cycle retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence.

The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association does not establish that two proteins do not interact. This release makes no final client assignment, isoform-preference claim, or ranked reference set.
