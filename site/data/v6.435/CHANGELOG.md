# Data v6.435 changes

## 2026-10-01

- Added HSP90AB1–IL6 support from PMID 42262456: HSP90AB1 overexpression raised secreted IL-6 in ox-LDL- and zingerone-treated primary human coronary endothelial cells. The paper describes this intervention inconsistently as “silencing” in some prose; the Methods and plotted groups show overexpression. The finding is specific to this treatment and does not establish binding or client status.
- Added HSP90B1–DNAJB11 support from PMID 35905823: DNAJB11-dependent BiP refolding follows GRP94 protection of heat-denatured luciferase in a purified system. The assay supports chaperone-system cooperation, not direct GRP94–DNAJB11 binding or a DNAJB11 client relationship.
- Added TRAP1–PINK1 genetic-rescue context from PMID 23525905: wild-type human TRAP1 overexpression partly rescues Pink1-loss phenotypes in flies and PINK1-silenced human cells. PMID 26631731 reports improvement after the opposite Trap1-loss intervention. Mechanism and dose response remain unresolved; the 2013 study does not newly establish binding or phosphorylation, and native supplement expression controls were unavailable.
- All three findings are support-only. They add no weighted score points, client claims, or final-reference-set promotion. Pair and network counts, reviewed cycle and co-chaperone component horizons, and affected HPA/CPTAC and private bulk-cycle withholding are unchanged.
