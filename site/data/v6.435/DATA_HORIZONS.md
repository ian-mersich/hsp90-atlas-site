# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.435

Data v6.435 contains 5,091 gene–isoform pairs and 4,125 reviewed source findings. Of the pairs, 2,792 have a reviewed source finding, 2,309 have pair-specific positive support, 137 are exclusion-only, and 2,299 remain discovery leads. The reviewed-with-support network contains 2,307 source-linked records. The three new findings are support-only; existing provisional scores, readiness, and final scored-reference-set status do not change.

HSP90AB1–IL6 PMID 42262456 reports increased secreted IL-6 after HSP90AB1 overexpression in ox-LDL- and zingerone-treated primary human coronary endothelial cells. The paper's “silencing” wording conflicts with its described and plotted overexpression groups. The result is treatment-specific secretion, not IL6 transcription, HSP90β–IL-6 binding, or client maturation. HSP90B1–DNAJB11 PMID 35905823 supports indirect GRP94–BiP–DNAJB11 cooperation in a purified, heat-denatured luciferase refolding assay; it does not establish binary GRP94–DNAJB11 binding or DNAJB11 client status. TRAP1–PINK1 PMID 23525905 shows partial rescue of PINK1-loss phenotypes by human TRAP1 overexpression in flies and human cells. A separate study, PMID 26631731, reports improvement after endogenous Trap1 loss; the opposite interventions do not establish a single dose-response mechanism. The 2013 paper did not newly assay PINK1–TRAP1 binding or phosphorylation, and its native supplement expression controls were unavailable for review.

Analytical profiles are carried forward from v6.109 over the v6.104 pair universe; affected HPA/CPTAC association summaries remain withheld. The private bulk-cycle database observations are excluded.
