# HSP90 Atlas release-candidate changelog

## Data v6.426 — 2026-10-01

This candidate retains all 4,115 v6.423 public source findings and adds one weak, support-only HSP90AA1–IL6 finding from PMID 41782509. In male Wistar rats with chronic cerebral hypoperfusion, the HSP90AA1-overexpression group had higher hippocampal IL-6 protein than the HLYN-only group (Fig. 8F). This is consistent with a downstream response in the tested model. The IL-6 panel omits the matched overexpression vector-control group shown elsewhere in the study.

IL-6 was measured by ELISA in whole hippocampus, with six rats per group. The study does not report IL6 RNA, an isolated-cell response, direct binding, a co-complex, or client dependence. The reviewed publisher manuscript is provisional and may differ from the final typeset article. This finding adds no score, client-mechanism, tier, or final-reference-set promotion. A separate context-only ACTB review (PMID 39048164) adds no public pair finding.

The core contains 5,091 pairs and 4,116 source findings; 2,790 pairs have reviewed findings, 2,307 have pair-specific positive support, 137 are exclusion-only, and 2,301 remain discovery leads. The source-backed network contains 2,305 relationships. Only the HSP90AA1–IL6 pair record changes relative to v6.423.
