# Data v6.433 changes

## 2026-10-01

- Added qualified support-only HSP90AB1–CNR2 co-purification from PMID 17698952. A published FLAG-CB2 immunoprecipitation band and MALDI-TOF peptide-mass fingerprint favor HSP90β, but the study did not report sequence spectra or an isoform-selective validation. Generic HSP90 co-IP and perturbations do not establish β-specific function, direct binding, or CB2 client maturation.
- Added a support-only HSP90AA1–IL6 response from PMID 38310993. LC3B-positive extracellular vesicles from hypoxia-conditioned HCC donor cells induced leukocyte IL-6 secretion; HSP90α antibody blockade and HSP90AA1 knockdown in HepG2 donors each partially reduced that response. The antibody's isoform specificity was not validated; one siRNA, no rescue or β comparator, mixed recipient leukocytes, and no EV-equivalence check limit causal interpretation. This is not HSP90α–IL-6 binding or client evidence.
- Neither new finding adds weighted-comparison points. The new CNR2–HSP90β pair has score 0 and is unranked; the IL6–HSP90α pair retains its pre-existing score and ranking eligibility from earlier, distinct sources. The reviewed cycle and co-chaperone component horizons are unchanged. Affected HPA/CPTAC association values and the private bulk-cycle observation layer remain withheld.
