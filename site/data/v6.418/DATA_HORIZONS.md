# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.418

Five new support-only findings come from three primary studies. Extracellular vesicle-surface Hsp90α blockade and TLR2/MyD88 loss affect tumor-autophagosome-driven IL-6 output in CD4 T cells (PMID 31300052). The HSP90AA1–IL6 finding concerns secretion; HSP90AA1–TLR2 is pathway context. Neither is evidence of direct Hsp90α–TLR2 binding or an intracellular client relationship.

Reported HSP90β knockdown in rat chondrocytes reduces IL-6 mRNA under inflammatory conditions (PMID 41795076). The supplement supports beta-targeting knockdown, but no alpha counter-assay or rescue establishes isoform exclusivity. The supplement labels its validation figure S6 despite the main-text S7 reference. HSP90AA1 and HSP90AB1 are each listed in a human A549 TRAF2-IP-MS dataset (PMID 36400972); co-recovery does not prove direct contact, client dependence, or compatible GRP94 topology. The earlier weak IL6 perturbation result from PMID 41567202 is retained once.
