# HSP90 Atlas release changelog

## Atlas v1.6.440 — 2026-10-03

Two qualified GRP94 findings from Poirier et al. (PMID 26628375) have been added to the existing PCSK9 and LDLR relationships. Crosslinked IP/MS, subsequent cellular co-immunoprecipitation and liver immunogold support GRP94–PCSK9 association, not purified binary binding or obligatory PCSK9 folding dependence. GRP94 protects LDLR abundance in the presence of PCSK9; this does not establish an LDLR client relationship. Cell-line, reagent-species, proximity, pooled-plasma and in-vivo mechanism limits are retained in the source records.

The release contains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,142 source findings. No pair, client assignment, numerical score or rank is added or promoted. Both new findings are visible but excluded from the frozen numerical comparison profiles.

The Interactive HSP90 Cycle adds one exact source for HSPA8–STIP1 cellular co-recovery (OpenCell, PMID 35271311). Its study context distinguishes IP-MS from binary binding or timed client transfer. Four other reviewed papers were not appended to existing edges because their purified or post-lysis assays support different claims. Graph structure and all prior source records are preserved.

The public application, reviewed evidence, Cycle source update and downloads use one current Atlas release. The structural Cycle model remains v6.303, its reviewed neighborhood v6.286 and the co-chaperone component v6.301. Analytical profiles retain the historical v6.109/v6.104 scope. Affected HPA/CPTAC summaries and the optional bulk database-observation layer remain withheld.
