# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.395

This release adds one HSP90β association identified in the original PPM1B supplementary proteomics table. The remaining current HSP90–gene, co-chaperone, and cycle findings are retained.

- The HSP90–gene core contains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,031 source findings. A source finding is a pair-specific evaluation, not an independent-paper count.
- In PMID 33048454, HSP90AB1/P08238 was recovered by HA-PPM1B immunoprecipitation followed by mass spectrometry in HL7702 cells. The result supports cellular co-recovery. It does not establish direct binding, co-chaperone function, or HSP90-dependent PPM1B maturation.
- The same article reports HSP90B-labelled phosphorylation responses after PPM1B knockdown and inhibitor treatment. Those array labels lack an accession or identifying peptide, so the phosphorylation sites are not assigned specifically to HSP90AB1 in this release.
- Three GRP94 association findings added in v6.394 remain qualified as modeled co-fractionation or BioID proximity. The v6.391 source-recheck corrections also remain intact.
- The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90 cycle retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence and contains no bulk unreviewed database observations.
- The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association does not establish that two proteins do not interact. This release makes no final client assignment, isoform-preference claim, or ranked reference set.
