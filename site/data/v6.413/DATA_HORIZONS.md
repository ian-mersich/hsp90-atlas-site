# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.413

Thirteen source findings add HSP90AA1- and HSP90AB1-resolved observations. Ten weak assay associations from PMID 33961781 retain their affinity-purification mass-spectrometry limitations. The tested genes are ANKK1, CCIN, DCAF13, GUCY2D, and NDUFAF2 for HSP90AA1, and ANKK1, DERL2, GBP2, NPAS1, and PNPLA5 for HSP90AB1. These measurements do not establish purified binary binding, client dependence, or isoform preference.

Two ITGB1BP2 findings from PMID 23414517 describe pairwise yeast two-hybrid interactions with the separately tested cytosolic isoforms. They remain supporting-only findings with the screen's model limitations. The EP300 finding from PMID 18559531 describes HSP90alpha acetylation by an upstream regulator; it does not establish an EP300 client relationship.
