# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.394

This release adds qualified GRP94 source observations while retaining the reviewed HSP90–gene evidence, co-chaperone network, and interactive HSP90 cycle from the preceding public release.

- The HSP90–gene core contains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,030 source records. Source records are pair-specific evaluations and are not a count of independent papers.
- Three source observations cover HSP90B1/GRP94 with DDOST and RPN2. PMID 26344197 reports modeled co-fractionation associations for both partners. PMID 34079125 reports RPN2-bait BioID proximity to HSP90B1 in HEK293 cells. These assays do not establish direct binding, a stable complex, or client maturation.
- The 155 corrected recheck decisions and nine reviewed findings introduced in v6.391 remain intact. A separate route ledger closes 13 cytosolic PubMed co-mentions that do not demonstrate their exact gene pairs; these decisions do not become interaction findings or change unrelated discovery evidence. One PPM1B route remains held pending the original supplementary hit table.
- The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90-cycle model retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence and does not include bulk unreviewed database observations.
- The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association or source finding does not establish that two proteins do not interact. Evidence profiles and network views are exploratory; this release does not assign final client status, definitive rankings, or pathway interpretations.
