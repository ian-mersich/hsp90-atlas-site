# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.400

This release adds ten source-reviewed TRAP1 assay associations with DBT, DLST, ETFA, GFM1, LRPPRC, MRPS26, PARK7, SHMT2, TACO1, and TRMT10C. Eight findings come from TRAP1-bait proximity labeling in HEK293 cells; two come from affinity-purification and immunoprecipitation mass spectrometry. The results support association in the reported assays. They do not establish direct binding, a stable complex, or TRAP1-dependent client maturation. The new findings remain unscored.

The public core retains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and contains 4,051 pair-specific source findings. Ten additional pairs have source review; the total is 2,735. Of those, 2,252 have pair-specific support. The reviewed interaction network contains 2,250 source-backed links. The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90 cycle retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence.

The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association does not establish that two proteins do not interact. This release makes no final client assignment, isoform-preference claim, or ranked reference set.
