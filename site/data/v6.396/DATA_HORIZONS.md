# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.396

This release corrects how source-reviewed findings determine the public evidence status of an HSP90–gene pair. It reuses the v6.395 source tables without adding or removing a paper finding.

- The HSP90–gene core retains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,031 pair-specific source findings. Of these pairs, 2,716 have at least one reviewed finding; the remaining 2,377 have discovery leads only.
- A reviewed finding that supplies biological or experimental context without supporting the exact pair is not presented as source support for that relationship. Of the 2,716 reviewed pairs, 2,233 have pair-specific support; 342 have only context or response evidence, four have only scoring-candidate findings, and 137 have only exclusionary findings. The public summary leads with a genuinely supportive finding when one exists.
- A source-supported pair is not necessarily isoform-resolved: evidence can establish an HSP90-family or shared cytosolic association while leaving HSP90α versus HSP90β unresolved. The pair detail retains the original assay scope and limitations.
- The reviewed interaction network contains 2,231 source-backed HSP90–gene links under the corrected support criterion. Among the 2,233 source-supported pairs, 318 have only assay-association findings; these are not evidence of direct physical binding or client maturation.
- The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90 cycle retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence and contains no bulk unreviewed database observations.
- The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association does not establish that two proteins do not interact. This release makes no final client assignment, isoform-preference claim, or ranked reference set.
