# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.429

Data v6.429 contains 5,091 gene–isoform pairs and 4,117 source findings: 2,791 pairs have reviewed findings, 2,308 have pair-specific positive support, and 2,300 remain discovery leads. The reviewed-with-support network contains 2,306 source-linked records; 2,305 were present before this release, and the new EGF relationship is visible as support-only with score 0 and no ranking eligibility.

The new HSP90AA1–EGF finding from PMID 24899266 describes EGF stimulation and pathway perturbations that regulate Hsp90α cell-surface localization in cultured human MDA-MB-231 cells. It does not establish EGF–HSP90AA1 binding, client dependence, or cochaperone function. The paper’s reported alpha-versus-beta antibody comparison in supplement panel S1E could not be visually inspected because the supplementary download was inaccessible; the local article package is absent. The finding is excluded from weighted comparisons and does not change any existing score dimensions or tiers.

The public reviewed cycle remains at its v6.303 horizon, and the co-chaperone companion remains at v6.301. Affected HPA/CPTAC association summaries remain withheld. No new pathway interpretation or final reference set is published.
