# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.397

This release adds ten source-reviewed assay associations for endoplasmic reticulum and mitochondrial proteins. Four findings concern HSP90B1/GRP94 with ERGIC1, ITIH3, SEC61B, and TM9SF4. Six concern TRAP1 with AFG3L2, DLD, DNAJA3, IARS2, and TIMM44; DLD has two independently reviewed findings. The cited measurements include affinity purification–mass spectrometry, proximity labeling, and one cross-link observation. They support assay associations in the reported systems, not direct binary binding or client dependence.

The public core retains 5,093 tracked gene–isoform pairs and now contains 4,041 pair-specific source findings. Nine additional pairs have source review; the total is 2,725. Of those, 2,242 have pair-specific support. The reviewed interaction network contains 2,240 source-backed links. The reviewed co-chaperone network retains its v6.301 evidence: 457 findings across 351 pairs. The interactive HSP90 cycle retains its v6.303 reviewed evidence.

The older HPA CPTAC association analysis used an incorrect value column. Its coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. The other seven HPA protein-profile datasets remain exploratory associations, not evidence of direct binding or client status.

Coverage is incomplete. A missing association does not establish that two proteins do not interact. This release makes no final client assignment, isoform-preference claim, or ranked reference set.
