# HSP90 Atlas Data v6.408

This release adds seven unscored TRAP1 assay associations reviewed in v6.407 and a cytosolic HSP90–NR1I2 source finding in v6.408. PXR is retained as the NR1I2 alias in search; duplicate PXR rows are merged into their canonical NR1I2 pairs. The reviewed records retain the reported experimental model, isoform scope, and limitations. Assay associations do not establish direct binding or client dependence, and shared cytosolic findings do not establish isoform preference.

The public core contains 5,091 tracked gene–isoform pairs and 4,072 pair-specific source findings. There are 2,755 source-reviewed pairs, including 2,272 with pair-specific support. The reviewed network contains 2,270 source-backed records. The reviewed co-chaperone companion retains its v6.301 evidence, with 457 findings across 351 pairs; the interactive HSP90 cycle retains its frozen v6.303 evidence.

The affected HPA CPTAC coefficients and derived top-hit labels remain withheld. Other HPA protein-profile associations remain discovery context. Coverage is incomplete, and absence of an association does not establish a biological negative. No final client assignment, isoform-preference conclusion, ranked reference set, or pathway interpretation is added.
