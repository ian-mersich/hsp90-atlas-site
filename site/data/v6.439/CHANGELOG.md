# HSP90 Atlas release changelog

## Data v6.439 candidate — 2026-10-02

This source-only update adds three qualified GRP94 findings from primary studies. In transfected HuH7 cells, GRP94 is recovered with PCSK9 by co-immunoprecipitation, particularly with an ER-retained PCSK9 variant (PMID 29593095). In FLT3-ITD AML models, kinase state is associated with cell-surface GRP94 abundance; quizartinib reduces the surface signal in surviving cells (PMID 41126722). In Chinese hamster ovary cells, HSPA5/GRP78 overexpression blunts stress-induced GRP94 mRNA (PMID 1373378).

The three findings add two tracked HSP90B1 pairs, PCSK9 and FLT3; HSP90B1–HSPA5 was already tracked. The PCSK9 result does not establish purified binding or GRP94-dependent PCSK9 folding. The FLT3 and HSPA5 findings are upstream regulatory observations in their reported models, not client or direct-binding claims. None enters the frozen numerical comparison profiles. The existing HSP90B1–LDLR finding remains visible but excluded from its historical two-finding score input.

The v6.303 reviewed-cycle and v6.301 co-chaperone horizons remain unchanged. Historical v6.109 HPA and BioGRID/IntAct profiles retain the v6.104 pair universe; the two new pairs have no detailed profile. Affected HPA/CPTAC summaries and private bulk-cycle observations remain withheld.
